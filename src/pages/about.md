TITANIUM 3DP is focused on titanium additive manufacturing for custom engineering components.

The site is built for engineers and purchasing teams who need to decide whether a titanium part is worth quoting through additive manufacturing. Typical inquiries involve Ti-6Al-4V, Ti-6Al-4V ELI, CP Ti, lightweight structures, internal channels, part consolidation, HIP, machining, inspection evidence, and traceability requirements.

## What We Do

We review drawings, CAD files, quantities, alloy targets, lead-time requirements, operating conditions, post-processing needs, inspection scope, and documentation expectations for titanium AM projects.

If the drawing and requirements are clear, we can quote from the information provided. If important details are missing, we ask focused clarification questions before quoting.

## What We Review

Common review points include material grade, LPBF or EBM process fit, DfAM limits, support removal access, distortion risk, machining allowance, surface finish, stress relief, HIP, CMM, CT, coupon requirements, density evidence, COA/COC, and traceability level.

The review depth depends on the part. A simple prototype may only need CAD, quantity, and material target. A function-critical part may need operating conditions, acceptance criteria, and inspection requirements before a useful quote can be prepared.

## What We Do Not Claim

Titanium additive manufacturing is not automatically the best route for every titanium part.

Simple geometry, tight all-over tolerances without machining access, high-volume cost sensitivity, or high-risk parts without an evidence budget may be better made by machining, casting, or forging.
