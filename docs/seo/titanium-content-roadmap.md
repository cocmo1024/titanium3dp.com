# Titanium3DP content system and expansion roadmap

Baseline date: 2026-07-24

## Objective

Build durable organic search coverage that produces qualified titanium additive manufacturing RFQs across multiple application fields without creating low-value keyword variants.

The business is not “publish as many titanium 3D printing pages as possible.” The business is:

1. help engineers decide whether a titanium part belongs in AM;
2. translate the decision into material, process, post-processing, inspection, and documentation requirements;
3. make supplier quotations comparable;
4. convert a controlled CAD and drawing package into a project review.

## Verified baseline

- 41 engineering articles existed before this roadmap.
- Average English word count was approximately 2,631.
- All 41 contained at least one internal link.
- 38 contained at least one external link.
- 40 contained a substantive update date.
- 24 titles followed the repeated `Titanium 3D Printing for X` construction.
- The existing tag system contained 173 tags, including 132 single-use tags, and generated 177 noindex tag pages.
- The new baseline adds one standards-and-specifications guide and maps the complete library into six durable decision paths.

Counts must be derived from the repository during future reviews. Do not manually carry these numbers forward as current facts.

## Durable topic architecture

| Decision path           | Search and buyer job                                                          | Current treatment                                                |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Engineering foundations | material, process, DfAM, post-processing, inspection, cost, lead time, RFQ    | Core guides plus materials, capabilities, knowledge and RFQ hubs |
| Flight and mobility     | aerospace, space, UAV, eVTOL, motorsport                                      | Application-specific risk and qualification guides               |
| Medical                 | Grade 23 ELI, porous or patient-specific structures, device boundary          | One medical decision guide plus the ELI material path            |
| Robotics and high tech  | robotics, semiconductor, cryogenic, data-center hardware                      | Equipment-level requirements translated into part decisions      |
| Energy and pressure     | hydrogen, fusion, geothermal, oil and gas, valves, manifolds, heat exchangers | Media, pressure, leak, internal geometry and inspection paths    |
| Corrosion and process   | chemical, seawater, marine, recycling, lithium, hydrometallurgy               | Chemistry, corrosion, erosion, deposits and process evidence     |

New posts must be assigned to exactly one primary decision path. Cross-links may connect secondary paths.

## Publishing gate

A new URL is approved only when all five answers are “yes”:

1. Does it resolve a distinct search task not already answered by a current URL?
2. Can the material, process, operating environment, standard, or acceptance boundary be named?
3. Can quantitative and high-risk claims be supported by authoritative or primary sources?
4. Can the page produce a purchasing output: an RFQ input, comparison rule, test requirement, or no-go decision?
5. Can at least one hub and two contextually related pages link to it at publication?

If the answer to any item is “no,” update an existing page or do not publish.

## Immediate inventory work

### P0: fix before scaling

- Add authoritative sources to the internal-channel, fatigue, and tolerance/CMM guides where high-risk or quantitative claims are currently unsupported.
- Compare the most overlapping corrosion/process pages using current query and page data before deciding to preserve, rewrite, merge, or redirect:
  - chemical processing vs. seawater desalination;
  - battery recycling vs. chemical processing;
  - carbon capture vs. geothermal.
- Replace repeated boilerplate with application-specific environment, failure, standard, inspection, and purchasing content.
- Rewrite repetitive `Titanium 3D Printing for X` titles when search data shows the page can own a more precise decision query. Preserve URLs.
- Keep the public tag route disabled. Use the six controlled decision paths instead.

### P1: high-value expansions after query validation

Candidate work is prioritized by buyer intent and evidence availability, not by headline novelty:

1. Update the standards map when ISO/ASTM 52941 Edition 2 is formally published.
2. Expand surface-finish and fatigue acceptance with authoritative method and surface-state sources.
3. Expand powder removal, cleanliness, and internal-channel validation with source-backed detectability and functional-test boundaries.
4. Build a joining and assembly decision guide only if search or RFQ evidence shows demand for welded, brazed, fastened, or hybrid titanium AM assemblies.
5. Build a dental or patient-matched device guide only when it can add device-specific regulatory and validation decisions beyond the existing medical page.
6. Build an application-specific commercial page only when it has a distinct service intent and cannot be served by the existing engineering article plus the application hub.

### P2: controlled topical expansion

- Standards and revision watch: ISO, ASTM, FDA, NASA and other governing sources.
- Search Console gap review: queries with impressions but weak CTR or missing landing-page fit.
- RFQ vocabulary review: recurring material, file, process, inspection, industry, and delivery terms from accepted inquiries.
- Supplier and production evidence review: publish only verified capability facts, never inferred equipment, certifications, customer cases, or results.

## Update cadence

### Monthly

- Review new queries, landing pages, accepted RFQs, and internal search terms.
- Fix broken links, stale standards status, and misleading snippets.
- Add contextual links from newly relevant pages.
- Record every published, merged, redirected, or rejected topic in the publication log.

### Quarterly

- Recalculate title, description, canonical, orphan-page, inbound-link, source, tag, and duplicate-passage metrics.
- Review the top 20 landing pages by impressions, clicks, RFQ assists, and accepted submissions.
- Audit emerging-technology pages for expired hype, unsupported claims, and weak commercial fit.
- Confirm material, process, inspection, and regulatory boundaries against current primary sources.

### Annually or after a material standard change

- Review every standard designation and status.
- Reconfirm supplier and production capability statements.
- Reassess redirects and consolidation decisions using at least 12 months of page/query evidence where available.

## Success metrics

Primary:

- accepted, non-spam RFQ submissions;
- qualified RFQs with CAD/drawings and enough information to quote;
- organic landing pages that assist an accepted RFQ;
- revenue or gross-margin contribution when the sales system can attribute it reliably.

Secondary:

- non-branded clicks and impressions across the six decision paths;
- share of target queries ranking in the top 10 and top 20;
- indexed pages with a unique decision intent;
- orphan pages and weak-inbound-link pages;
- accepted-form conversion rate by landing page and cluster.

Do not optimize around raw indexed-page count, raw tag count, word count, or traffic that cannot plausibly produce the target titanium-part inquiry.

## Failure and exit rules

- Stop a proposed page when it is a material or industry name substituted into an existing template.
- Merge or redirect only after page/query evidence is reviewed; do not delete a ranking URL because its prose looks repetitive.
- Remove or qualify a claim immediately when its source, supplier, equipment, certification, or customer evidence cannot be verified.
- Roll back a navigation or conversion change if accepted RFQs fall materially and the decline is attributable to the change.
- Pause expansion if technical SEO, secure RFQ delivery, or production verification is failing.
